Latte Panda: a mobile robot that sorts cups and collects donations

Latte Panda is a service robot for public recycling. It finds a person holding a disposable cup, sorts the cup into a paper or plastic bin, and invites a donation through a QR code.

Team
6 students
Compute
LattePanda Alpha, Jetson TX2, Arduino Mega
Sensors
RealSense D435, touchscreen
Stack
ROS, C++, YOLOv5, TensorRT, PyQt
Watch the demo video

What a visit looks like

  1. 1 Approach The robot drives around, finds a person and greets them with a motion and a short introduction.
  2. 2 Sort It asks for a disposable cup, recognizes it as paper or plastic, and tilts the matching bin open.
  3. 3 Donate The touchscreen offers a donation by QR code and plays a thank-you video.

The system

DetectionColor frames go to a YOLO server, which sends them to the Jetson TX2 with a mode: find people, classify a cup as paper or plastic, or read a hand sign. YOLOv5 ran on the TX2 with TensorRT.
Obstacle avoidanceThe 640×480 depth image is low-pass filtered, averaged over 20 frames and split into a 3×5 grid. The nearest distance in each cell, weighted by a sigmoid, sets the left and right wheel speeds.
Following a personBearing comes from the horizontal position of the detection; distance is the mean depth over a 100×100 window at its center.
ActuationDynamixel motors play the robot's greeting motions and tilt the bins; an Arduino Mega runs the drive motors with PID control over rosserial.

My part

On a team of six I worked on the system design, motor control, communication, sensor control, the algorithms, the electrical system and the UI.
